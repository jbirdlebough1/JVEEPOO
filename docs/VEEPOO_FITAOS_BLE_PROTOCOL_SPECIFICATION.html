1. Wireless Architecture & GATT Profile

The Veepoo Model 5010 and FITAOS smartwatch architecture communicates over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE 5.0) using dedicated primary GATT services. All control frames, query requests, and streaming telemetry packets conform to a strict 20-byte payload boundary. Commands sent from the host computer to the watch (TX) and notifications received from the watch (RX) must be framed and padded to exactly 20 bytes.

GATT Transmission Rule & FIFO Queue Serialization
Windows and Chrome Web Bluetooth strictly disallow concurrent GATT write operations. If a command is transmitted while an existing write is in flight, the browser aborts with an uncaught DOMException. The LCARS communications layer serializes all GATT operations through a single FIFO Promise queue with a mandatory 65-millisecond inter-packet spacing window.

4. Active Watch Procedures vs. Automated Background Harvesting

A critical operational distinction exists between biomarkers that are harvested autonomously in the background versus clinical tests that require specific active physical procedures by the patient.

A. Lead-I Electrocardiogram (ECG) Procedure

Physical Requirements for Valid Lead-I ECG Capture
Because an electrocardiogram measures microvolt electrical potentials produced by myocardial depolarization across the chest cavity (Einthoven Lead-I axis: right arm to left arm), the watch cannot measure ECG using the bottom case optical sensor alone. An active electrical circuit across the chest must be formed.

Step-by-Step Patient Procedure:

  1. Arm & Posture Stability: Sit comfortably in a chair with arms resting on a table. Keep breathing regular and refrain from talking or moving.
  2. Initiate Test on Watch Face: Swipe to the ECG menu on the smartwatch face and tap the green Start button (or click COMMENCE SCAN in LCARS Tab 03 Continuous Cardio).
  3. Close Electrical Circuit: Place the thumb and index finger of your opposite hand firmly onto the curved stainless steel electrode on the right bezel of the watch case. Ensure the watch back is in snug contact with the wrist of the wearing arm.
  4. Maintain Contact for 30 Seconds: Keep continuous contact for 30 seconds. The watch screen displays a live rhythm tracing. When the test completes, the watch delivers a haptic vibration pulse and displays heart rhythm diagnosis and QTc interval duration.
  5. Ingest into LCARS Vault: When the watch syncs via Bluetooth, LCARS ingests the digitized QTc interval and plots it in Sub-Report 02.

B. Blood Composition & Viscosity Tonometry Procedure

Optical Microvascular Rheology Protocol
Blood composition measurements evaluate optical dispersion across green, red, and infrared wavelengths to estimate plasma viscosity (cP), total lipid proxy (mg/dL), and serum uric acid indicator (mg/dL).

Patient Workflow:

  • Fasting Baseline: For optimal clinical comparability, conduct blood composition tests in the morning upon waking, prior to food intake.
  • Execution: On the watch, navigate to the Blood Composition / Uric Acid menu and tap Start. Rest the wrist motionless on a flat surface for 30 seconds while the multi-wavelength optical array completes its sweep.
  • Automated Mode: If Blood Component Detection is enabled in 05-SETUP (Opcode 0xB8 Page 1, byte 11 = 1), the watch automatically performs background scheduled sweeps during periods of rest.

C. Automated Background Harvesting (Zero Patient Action Required)

Fully Autonomous 24/7 Biometric Derivation
The following metrics are collected continuously by the watch firmware without requiring any manual test initiation:
  • MET (Metabolic Equivalent of Task) Energy Expenditure: Continuously calculated by firmware integration of the triaxial accelerometer and heart rate dynamics (`0xB8 Page 1, byte 8 = 1`). Cumulative daily MET-hours are tallied automatically to evaluate ADA compliance (≥ 28 MET-hrs/day).
  • Continuous Optical Glucose (CGM): Periodic spectrographic scans execute automatically every 10 to 15 minutes when glucoseAuto is enabled (`0xB8 Page 1, byte 7 = 1`), establishing the diurnal glycemic trajectory.
  • Ambulatory Blood Pressure & Dynamic Pulse: Oscillometric pressure pulse transit time and heart rate track at automated intervals (`0xB8 Page 0, bytes 4 and 5`).
  • Dual-Sensor Temperature: Core and skin temperatures update automatically every 10 minutes (`0xB8 Page 1, byte 4`).
  • Nocturnal Sleep Hypnogram: Actigraphy and cardiopulmonary coupling record automatically when the watch detects sleep onset, archiving light, deep, and REM cycles into non-volatile memory for retrieval via Opcode 0xE0.

6. Watch Face Memory, SPI Flash & OTA Binary Architecture

The Veepoo / FITAOS smartwatch contains high-capacity NOR SPI flash memory partitioned for user-customizable watch dials. Dial images are rendered as 240x280 pixel bitmaps with 16-bit RGB565 color depth (134,400 raw bytes).

  • Slot 0 (Firmware ROM Default): Factory built-in analog dial hard-coded in system flash.
  • Slot 1 (Sport Multi-Function): Stock digital dial featuring real-time step counter, calorie ring, and heart rate bay.
  • Slot 2 (Executive Digital): Stock minimalist high-contrast clinical dial.
  • Slot 3 (SPI Flash User Dial): Dedicated rewritable partition (starting at address 0x00020000, capacity 134,400 bytes) allocated for OTA wireless dial installation.

Dial binary files package a proprietary 6-byte Veepoo container header (0x56 0x50 0x01 ...) followed by RGB565 pixel streams. Transmission occurs over Characteristic 0000fec7 in paced 20-byte chunks. Upon completion of all sectors, the host transmits a 16-bit CRC checksum (CCITT-FALSE). The watch MCU calculates the on-chip CRC across flash memory; if verified, it engages the new dial via Opcode 0xC7 0x01 0x03 without requiring a device reboot.
