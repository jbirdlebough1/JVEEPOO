LIMITATIONS OF CONVENTIONAL SPOT-CHECKS

Examining why intermittent spot-checks fail to capture metabolic kinetics, excursions, and nocturnal hypoglycemia.

  • 90-Day A1C Snapshot Aliasing: A quarterly lab A1C is an isolated N=1 sample every 90 days, suffering complete Nyquist aliasing and masking all diurnal peaks and nocturnal nadirs.
  • Nyquist Sampling & Statistical Power: Information theory requires sampling at twice the biological kinetic frequency; a single snapshot has zero statistical confidence compared to continuous telemetry.
  • LCARS 288×8 Continuous Telemetry: Capturing 288 samples/day across 8 channels yields 2,304 daily metrics—over 25,920 glucose points and 207,360 total biometric measurements in a 90-day cycle.
