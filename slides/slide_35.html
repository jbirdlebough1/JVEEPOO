ADAG MATHEMATICAL ESTIMATED A1C PROJECTION ENGINE

Translating continuous glucose telemetry into rolling estimated A1C projections via the ADAG consensus algorithm.

  • ADAG Mathematical Formulation: eA1C = (Glucose + 46.7) / 28.7 translates optical telemetry directly to percentage scale.
  • Continuous Trajectory Tracking: Detects therapeutic efficacy and dietary improvements within days instead of quarterly delays.
  • Physician Baseline Anchor: Anchored to the patient's in-clinic venous blood draw for precise laboratory calibration.

ADAG CONSENSUS EQUATION (DIABETES CARE 2008) eA1C MODEL
1. ROLLING ESTIMATED A1C FORMULA:
eA1C (%) = (Mean Glucose + 46.7) / 28.7
2. INVERSE MEAN GLUCOSE EQUIVALENT:
Mean Glucose (mg/dL) = (28.7 × eA1C) - 46.7
SENSITIVITY DERIVATIVE:
d(eA1C) / d(MeanG) = 1 / 28.7 ≈ 0.0348% per mg/dL
➔ Every -28.7 mg/dL sustained drop lowers eA1C by exactly -1.0%.
