AUTONOMIC HRV SDNN & SYMPATHO-VAGAL EQUILIBRIUM

Heart rate variability analysis using SDNN and Lorenz scatter plots to audit sympatho-vagal autonomic balance.

  • Millisecond R-R Interval Variation: Calculates SDNN standard deviation to quantify autonomic nervous system resilience.
  • Sympatho-Vagal Balance Assessment: Distinguishes between restorative parasympathetic tone and sympathetic fight-or-flight stress.
  • Lorenz Scatter Diagnostic Dynamics: Provides a 2D geometric scatter visual of cardiac rhythm stability and autonomic fatigue.
AUTONOMIC HRV & LORENZ SCATTER MATHEMATICS POINCARÉ GEOMETRY (SD1 / SD2)
HRV TIME-DOMAIN & POINCARÉ GEOMETRY AUTONOMIC TONE
1. TIME-DOMAIN STANDARD METRICS:
• Inter-Beat Interval: RR_i = t_i - t_{i-1} (ms)
• Total Autonomic Power: SDNN = √[ 1/(N-1) ∑ (RR_i - Mean_RR)² ]
• Parasympathetic Vagal Tone: RMSSD = √[ 1/(N-1) ∑ (RR_{i+1} - RR_i)² ]
2. LORENZ 2D SCATTER ELLIPSE EQUATIONS (RR_i vs RR_{i+1}):
• Transverse Axis (Instantaneous Vagal):
  SD1 = √[ 1/2 Var(RR_{i+1} - RR_i) ] = RMSSD / √2
• Longitudinal Axis (Total / Sympathetic):
  SD2 = √[ 2·SDNN² - 1/2·RMSSD² ]
• Sympatho-Vagal Balance Ratio:
  Ratio = SD1 / SD2 (Normal resting ratio: 0.45 – 0.55)
CLINICAL MORPHOLOGY DIAGNOSTIC:
• Comet Shape (Normal):
Wide dispersion, robust parasympathetic vagal recovery (SD1 > 25 ms).
• Constricted Torpedo (Diabetic Neuropathy):
Narrow scatter along identity line (SD1 < 12 ms), indicating autonomic exhaustion and cardiovascular vulnerability.
Key Takeaway: Quantifies autonomic nervous system reserve, screening for diabetic neuropathy and chronic stress.
