LIMITATIONS OF CONVENTIONAL SPOT-CHECKS

Examining why intermittent spot-checks fail to capture metabolic kinetics, excursions, and nocturnal hypoglycemia.

  • Postprandial Excursions: Spot checks miss the peak post-meal glucose excursions that drive diabetic complications.

Key Takeaway: Continuous monitoring captures critical glycemic dynamics that single spot-checks completely miss.
