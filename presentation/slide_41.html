2. THE FOUR HYPNOGRAM STAGES & CLINICAL BENCHMARKS:
• Deep Delta Sleep (N3): Target 1.5–2.0h (>20%). Slow-wave delta rhythms suppress pituitary ACTH, blunting overnight cortisol and re-sensitizing muscle GLUT4 insulin receptors.
• Light Sleep (N1/N2): 50%–60% of total rest; physiological baseline rest.
• REM Dreaming (R): 20%–25%; autonomic and neurovascular restoration.
• Awake Epochs: <5%; minimal micro-arousals.
