HOW THE FITAOS 5010 SENSORS WORK PATIENT & CLINICIAN WALKTHROUGH
Multi-Wavelength Optical LEDs + Photodiode Window: Green (530 nm), Red (660 nm), and Infrared (940 nm) light pulses penetrate wrist capillaries safely; reflected light measures optical glucose, pulse, HRV, PWTT blood pressure, and blood oxygen.
Metallic Rear Pads & Side Bezel Electrode: Wrist skin contacts plus your index finger resting 30 seconds on the metallic side bezel complete a closed bio-electrical loop for Lead-I ECG, QTc intervals, and body composition (BIA).
Precision Metallic Contact Thermistor & Motion: Measures skin & estimated core temperature continuously every five minutes while accelerometer bursts capture post-meal steps and sleep cycles without needles.
Key Takeaway: Combining three optical LED wavelengths, metallic ECG/BIA electrodes, and a contact thermistor lets one comfortable FITAOS 5010 watch capture 12 clinical streams pain-free.
