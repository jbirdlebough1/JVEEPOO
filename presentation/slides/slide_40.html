2D LORENZ & POINCARÉ MATHEMATICS CLINICAL PARITY (SD1 / SD2)
OEM App 2D POINCARÉ & TIME-DOMAIN FORMULAS CLINICAL SPEC
1. TIME-DOMAIN STANDARD METRICS:
• Beat Interval: RR_i = t_i - t_{i-1} (ms)
• Total Autonomic Power: SDNN = √[ 1/(N-1) ∑ (RR_i - Mean_RR)² ]
• Parasympathetic Vagal Tone: RMSSD = √[ 1/(N-1) ∑ (RR_{i+1} - RR_i)² ]
2. LORENZ 2D SCATTER ELLIPSE EQUATIONS (RR_i vs RR_{i+1}):
• Transverse Axis (Instantaneous Vagal):
  SD1 = √[ 1/2 Var(RR_{i+1} - RR_i) ] = RMSSD / √2
• Longitudinal Axis (Total / Sympathetic):
  SD2 = √[ 2·SDNN² - 1/2·RMSSD² ]
• Sympatho-Vagal Balance Ratio:
  Ratio = SD1 / SD2 (Normal resting ratio: 0.30 – 0.50)
5-ZONE MORPHOLOGICAL CLASSIFICATION MATRIX:
✨ COMET (NORMAL) Wide fan, robust vagal recovery (SD1 > 25 ms, Score: 88).
⚡ TORPEDO (FATIGUE) Narrow diagonal cigar; autonomic neuropathy & exhaustion (SD1 < 10 ms).
⚪ CLUB (RIGID) Compact round cluster; low autonomic plasticity & tachycardia.
🔱 FORK (BIFURCATED) Parallel wings; premature ventricular contractions (PVCs) / bigeminy.
🌐 COMPLEX (AFIB) Chaotic multi-cluster scatter; atrial fibrillation & high ectopy.
